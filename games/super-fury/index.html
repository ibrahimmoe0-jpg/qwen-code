Unofficial fan game in the style of Dragon Ball Z: Buu's Fury, following the Dragon Ball Super sagas. Goku's sprites and the dialog portraits are from the GBA game via spritedatabase.net (Goku ripped by Grim, Super Saiyan by ApplePie, Super Saiyan 3 by Dx-Yondy, portraits by Omega); all other pixel art is original.
